Any evening · Breakaway Reel (a short video)

On your phone, open stickpicks.hockey/intermission.html and start a screen recording (iPhone: Control Center → Screen Recording; Android: Quick Settings → Screen record). Play for 15–30 seconds. In Instagram: New → Reel → choose the recording → trim → Next → Edit cover → Add from camera roll → choose this picture → paste the caption.

3 · Keeping it going

  • Game nights: ask Claude for "an Instagram game-night picture for [date]". It's made from the real schedule.
  • Every Press Box update gets an Instagram picture and caption too, as part of the update (style guide section 7).
  • Reply to comments, and follow and comment on team and hockey accounts you like; that's how new accounts get found.
  • Never use NHL or team logos; team names in words are fine.
