1 · Create the account

  1. Sign up at bsky.app (free). Use contact@stickpicks.hockey as the email.
  2. Display name: stickpicks
  3. Profile picture: the stickpicks app icon (the “sp” script on knit). Use icon-512.png from the site.
  4. Banner: bluesky-banner.png (in this marketing folder; made at Bluesky's 1500×500 size).
  5. Bio (164/256 characters):
    Free NHL pick'em 🏒 Call every game (W, L or OTL + total goals) and climb your team's own leaderboard. No money, no betting, no ads. Made by a fan. stickpicks.hockey

2 · Use your domain as your name: @stickpicks.hockey

  1. In Bluesky: Settings → Account → Handle → “I have my own domain”, and type stickpicks.hockey.
  2. Bluesky shows a TXT record with Host _atproto and a value starting did=did:plc:….
  3. In Porkbun: Domain Management → stickpicks.hockey → DNS → Add a record: Type TXT, Host _atproto, Answer: paste the did=… value. Leave every existing record alone.
  4. Back in Bluesky, press Verify DNS Record, then Update. Your name becomes @stickpicks.hockey.

It's the same kind of step as the Google verification. I can check from here that the record is visible once you've added it.
